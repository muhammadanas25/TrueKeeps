# Truekeeps Brand Guidelines (v1, Nov 2026)

## For Claude: how to use this kit
This folder is the official Truekeeps brand. When designing anything for Truekeeps (website, social posts, packaging, slides, mockups):
- Use only the colours in this file and in `colours/tokens.css` / `colours/tokens.json`.
- Use the logo files in `logos/` exactly as given. Never redraw or retype the logo.
- Body font is Figtree (free on Google Fonts). Heading font is not decided yet: use Figtree ExtraBold (800) as a stand-in.
- `Truekeeps-Brand-Guidelines-Visual.pdf` shows every rule visually.
- `colours/Truekeeps-Colours.ase` is the swatch library for Illustrator, Photoshop and InDesign.

## Brand feel
Warm, emotional, clean. Like opening a family album. Customer photos are always the hero.

## Logo
- Two parts: tk mark (square) + "truekeeps" wordmark. Always use original artwork; never retype in a font.
- Allowed colours: Indigo on Paper/white (primary), white on Indigo or dark photos (reversed), black (one-colour print only).
- Mark only for small spaces (favicon, Instagram profile, book spine, stickers). White mark on Rose allowed for mark only.
- Clear space: x = half the height of the tk square, on all sides.
- Minimum size: full logo 120 px / 30 mm; mark 24 px / 7 mm.
- Don't: stretch, recolour (no Teal/Rose logo), rotate, add shadows/outlines, place on busy photo without dark overlay.

## Colours (roles)
| Name | HEX | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Indigo | #2A1E5C | Primary brand dark: logo, headings, packaging, dark sections |
| Rose | #EE4266 | Accent / emotion: highlights, big headlines, graphics, print |
| Rose 600 | #CA3A58 | All buttons (white text passes readability; Rose 500 does not for small text) |
| Rose 700 | #A5324A | Rose-coloured small text, button hover |
| Paper | #FBF6F0 | Main background (warm off-white) |
| Teal | #3CBBB1 | Success only (order confirmed, delivered). Max ~5%. Never next to big Rose areas |
| Mist | #C4CBCA | Lines, borders, placeholders, quiet panels |
| Ink | #0A0F0D | Body text only, not a brand colour on its own |

Ratio: ~60% Paper/white, 25-30% Indigo, 10% Rose, up to 5% Teal/Mist.

Shade scales (50 to 900):
- Rose: FEF0F3, FCDDE3, F9BBC8, F595A9, F26C88, EE4266, CA3A58, A5324A, 812A3B, 5C212D
- Indigo: EEEDF2, D9D6E2, B2AEC4, 8881A4, 595080, 2A1E5C, 251C4F, 201943, 1B1736, 161429
- Teal: EFFAF9, DCF3F1, B9E7E3, 92D9D3, 67CAC2, 3CBBB1, 349F97, 2C847D, 246862, 1C4D48
- Mist: FAFBFB, F4F6F5, EAECEC, DEE2E1, D1D6D6, C4CBCA, A6ADAC, 888F8E, 6B716F, 4D5351
- Ink (50-500): EBECEC, D3D4D3, A7A9A8, 767977, 404442, 0A0F0D

Readability: Indigo on Paper and white on Indigo are safe for all text. White on Rose 500 = big bold text only. Teal and Mist never for text on light backgrounds. Never Teal on Rose.

Print (CMYK starting points, confirm Pantone + test print with printer): Indigo 54/67/0/64, Rose 0/72/57/7, Rose 600 0/71/56/21, Teal 68/0/5/27 (high risk of printing dull), Mist 3/0/0/20, Ink rich black 60/40/40/100 (use 0/0/0/100 for small text).

## Typography
- Body: Figtree (400 body, 500 links/nav, 600 labels/badges, 700 buttons/card titles).
- Heading font: TO BE DECIDED. Stand-in: Figtree ExtraBold 800, sentence case.
- Web scale: H1 48/56, H2 32/40, H3 20/28, body 16/26, small 14/22, eyebrow 12 px caps +16% letter spacing.
- Headings in Indigo; body in Ink (~85%) or Indigo 400.

## UI
- One main action per screen: Rose 600 pill button, label "Make my book". Secondary: Indigo outline. Tertiary: underlined link. On dark: Rose main + white secondary.
- Badges: Teal 100/900 = success, Rose 50/700 = new/offer, Indigo 50 = info.
- Selected states and step numbers in Indigo (no separate bright blue).
- Signature device: Rose highlight block behind the one most emotional phrase, headlines only, max once per screen.
- Radius: buttons and badges fully round, cards 14px.

## Photography
Warm natural light, real Pakistani families and moments, hands holding the book. Text on photos needs an Indigo 900 gradient overlay. Avoid cold light, heavy filters, Western stock.

## Voice
Warm friend, simple words, short sentences, no pressure. Avoid jargon ("bespoke solutions", "AI-powered engine") and hype ("Hurry! Order NOW!!!").

## Website alignment (open items, Nov 2026)
- Royal blue #163491 ("Start free", step numbers) -> Indigo
- Electric blue #0138FF (selected occasion) -> Indigo border + Indigo 50 fill
- Yellow #F1CC2C (eyebrow dash, icons) -> Rose (open decision: or make yellow official and drop Teal)
- Charcoal #2E2E2D (headings, trust bar) -> Indigo / Indigo 50
- Pink #CB1657 button -> Rose 600
- Background #F9F9F5 -> Paper
- Use one CTA label and colour everywhere ("Make my book").
- Trust bar claims to verify or remove before launch: "4.9/5 from 12,000 reviews", "Love-it guarantee" (conflicts with no refunds once printing starts), "Free shipping from Rs 10,000", "FSC-certified paper".
